On 23 October 1956, music and nervous laughter fill the birthday ball held for Svetlana, daughter of the local Russian commander, in a small settlement near the border. Beneath the official celebration, the town’s young people prepare to strike at the local ÁVÓ men, whose presence has turned ordinary life into a routine of intimidation, suspicion, and enforced obedience. Among the guests, Juli, the daughter of the Hungarian ÁVÓ commander, is drawn to Robi, the son of an actor deported from Budapest. Their attraction quickly becomes a private act of defiance: each sees in the other a life beyond the loyalties, fear, and inherited divisions imposed by their families.

The planned confrontation is overtaken by electrifying news from Budapest: revolution has broken out. The ball collapses into confusion as the possibility of freedom reaches even this isolated town. Old authority begins to look fragile, young people abandon caution, and Robi and Juli cling to the belief that their love might survive the upheaval. Around them, friends, parents, officers, and soldiers are forced to choose whether to protect themselves, preserve the old order, or risk everything for change.

Yet the widening revolt sharpens rather than erases the forces separating the lovers. Political violence enters their intimate world, personal grudges become dangerous, and the hopeful momentum of the uprising gives way to betrayal, loss, and the brutal consequences of power. Robi and Juli’s attempt to cross the boundary between enemy households becomes inseparable from the town’s struggle to escape repression, carrying their story toward tragedy.

The musical places youthful love beside a people’s longing for freedom, showing how private happiness can be crushed by political terror and collective conflict. It also honours courage, solidarity, and the painful cost borne by those who dare to imagine another future.

Eke Máté slips through the countryside around Siklós castle, appearing wherever the labanc occupation is weakest and vanishing before its soldiers can seize him. Known as the Captain of the Tenkes, he turns disguise, bluff and quick thinking into weapons for the kuruc cause, while his love for Veronika gives his dangerous work a personal stake. At the local inn, Dudva provides a meeting place where rumours travel quickly, and Rózsa uses her wit and mobility to help Máté stay a step ahead of the enemy.

Inside the castle, colonel Eberstein Echbert tries to impose order, but his authority is repeatedly undermined by Máté’s audacious intrusions. Amália, Eberstein Echbert’s wife, is drawn into the confusion created by the elusive rebel, while sergeant Pityik and captain Bruckenbacker Alfonz pursue clues that continually lead them astray. Siklósi bácsi, Buga Jakab and the people of the surrounding area become part of a growing web of resistance, protecting one another and exploiting the occupiers’ arrogance.

As pressure mounts, brigadier Béri Balogh Ádám needs intelligence and a practical opening against the labanc forces. Máté accepts increasingly perilous assignments: he infiltrates enemy territory, disrupts their plans and transforms apparent setbacks into opportunities. The arrival of the General and Helén, his wife, raises the stakes, bringing the conflict between command, loyalty and private feeling into sharper focus. Yet Eberstein Echbert’s attempts to trap the Captain of the Tenkes only reveal how little he understands the land and the people he seeks to govern. In the decisive confrontation, Máté’s courage and ingenuity help the kuruc side turn resistance into action.

The musical celebrates freedom won through solidarity, nerve and imagination. Its adventure and comic misunderstandings contrast the occupiers’ rigid power with the resourcefulness of ordinary people who refuse to live as servants.

羅比在匈牙利邊境小鎮的舞會上闖入人群，與公安祕密警察指揮官之女茱莉相遇；鎮上年輕人原本正密謀反抗長年高壓統治。1956年10月23日，首都爆發革命的消息傳來，俄國指揮官女兒的生日宴猝然中斷，原訂的衝突也被更巨大的動亂吞沒。羅比是自首都遭放逐的演員之子，茱莉則出身掌權者家庭，兩人的戀情從一開始就橫跨了身分、家族與政治陣線。

隨著革命浪潮蔓延，原先彼此猜忌、對立的居民被迫表態；青年們嚮往自由，也在槍口、告密與報復的陰影下尋找彼此。神父洛林試圖阻止衝突惡化，卻無法消弭鎮內累積已久的仇恨。羅比與茱莉想擺脫父輩留下的敵意，將愛情視為逃離恐懼的出口；然而，兩人愈靠近，愈被捲入革命與鎮上權力鬥爭的中心。

當局勢由解放的狂喜轉為殘酷清算，私人的誤解與政治暴力交織，讓相愛的兩人失去選擇未來的餘地。命運最終沒有讓他們跨越陣營，而使兩名無辜的年輕人一同成為時代事件的犧牲者。

在血與失落之後，羅比與茱莉的愛情留下的不是勝利，而是一個小鎮對自由、恐懼與代價的共同記憶。那56滴血，既是革命的傷痕，也是青春在歷史洪流中被奪走的證言。

在西元1704年、錫克洛什城堡附近的滕凱什山，機智勇敢的義軍首領艾凱．馬泰率領鄉民，持續與駐守當地的哈布斯堡軍周旋。奧軍上校埃伯斯坦．艾希貝特以嚴刑與搜捕鎮壓反抗，卻屢次被人稱「滕凱什隊長」的馬泰戲弄；他的妻子阿瑪莉亞雖身處敵營，也看見丈夫的傲慢與殘酷。馬泰在維羅妮卡、錫克洛什大叔及布加．雅各布等人的掩護下，往返村落、酒館與山林傳遞消息，並與酒館老闆杜德瓦、吉普賽少女羅莎共同設局，讓敵軍疲於奔命。

貝里．鮑洛格．亞當准將率義軍策畫奪回要地，馬泰則把看似零散的惡作劇化為戰術：設陷阱、偷換命令、假扮身分，使敵方士兵彼提克與布魯肯巴克．阿爾豐茲上尉一次次出糗。當將軍偕妻子海倫前來督戰，埃伯斯坦急於立功，反而因誤判局勢而使防線鬆動；馬泰遂帶領眾人把握機會，協助義軍扭轉戰局。

在笑料與驚險交錯的行動中，馬泰證明真正的勇敢不只在於武力，更在於守護同伴、相信自由。滕凱什山上的反抗，也因此凝聚成所有人不願屈服的共同力量。

1956年10月23日，匈牙利边境小镇笼罩在恐惧与监控之下，秘密警察机关为苏军指挥官的女儿举办舞会。年轻人借机策划袭击当地武装人员，盼望打破沉闷的统治；谁知首都爆发革命的消息骤然传来，舞会被迫中断，原本隐秘的反抗也被推入更危险的局面。

舞会的混乱中，秘密警察指挥官的女儿尤丽与被从布达佩斯流放到此的演员之子罗比相识相爱。她生长在权力与秩序的一边，他则背负父辈受迫害的阴影；两人的结合不仅触犯家庭立场，也卷入小镇青年对自由的渴望。革命的风暴席卷而至，朋友、亲人和恋人都不得不在忠诚、恐惧、良知与生存之间作出选择。

随着冲突升级，爱情不再只是两个人的私事，而成为对暴力和仇恨的抵抗。罗比与尤丽试图守住彼此，却无法逃离时代强加的阵营与代价；原先带着青春冲动和玩笑色彩的行动，终于显露出血与牺牲的重量。

全剧以一对相爱的年轻人为中心，凝望1956年革命中的普通人。它让爱情与自由彼此映照，也让悲剧指向压迫之下仍不肯熄灭的人性与希望。
